Whitening is the treatment we get asked about most and it is the one where honesty matters most, because the marketing around it is loud.

## What it does

Professional whitening lightens the natural colour of the enamel by a few shades using a peroxide gel. It works on healthy teeth with surface staining from coffee, tea, tobacco, or age.

## What it does not do

It does not change the colour of fillings, crowns, or veneers. If you whiten your natural teeth, any existing restorations will now look darker than the teeth around them. This is often a reason we suggest waiting, or replacing a visible restoration afterwards.

## When we say no

If your gums are inflamed, if you have sensitivity that has not been investigated, or if the discolouration is coming from inside the tooth rather than the surface — whitening is not the right treatment and we will tell you that.

An honest opinion is worth more than the fee for a procedure that will not do what you hoped it would.
