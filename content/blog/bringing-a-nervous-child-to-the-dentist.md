Most children arrive at their first dental visit with whatever picture of the dentist the adults around them have painted. A few small choices in the weeks before change how the appointment goes.

## Do not promise it will not hurt

You cannot promise this and if it turns out you are wrong, they will remember. Say instead: "The dentist will look at your teeth. If anything is uncomfortable, you can put your hand up and they will stop."

## Book the first visit as a look-only

Ask us for a twenty-minute appointment with no treatment planned. The child sits in the chair, meets the dentist, gets a ride up and down, and leaves. That is the whole appointment. The next one is easier because of it.

## Come at a good time of day

Not when they are hungry, not when they are due a nap. Morning appointments before school are usually better than after.

## Bring one comfort item, not a bag

A soft toy, a familiar blanket. Put phones and tablets away — a screen crowds out the calm.
