---
title: What to do if a tooth is knocked out
description: A knocked-out adult tooth can often be saved if you act in the first thirty minutes. Here is what to do.
date: 2026-06-14
author: Dr. [Clinician one]
---

A knocked-out adult tooth can often be saved if it is put back in its socket within thirty minutes. Baby teeth are not replanted — call us for advice, but do not push them back in.

## Pick the tooth up by the crown

The crown is the white part you normally see. Do not touch the root — the fibres on it are what let it re-attach.

## Rinse it briefly if it is dirty

Milk, saline, or a very short rinse under cold tap water. Do not scrub it. Do not use soap.

## Put it back in the socket if you can

Push it in gently and hold it there by biting on a clean cloth. If you cannot, put the tooth in a cup of milk — not water, not tissue paper.

## Come to us now

Call the practice or the emergency line. Bring the tooth. Time is the single biggest factor in whether we can save it.
